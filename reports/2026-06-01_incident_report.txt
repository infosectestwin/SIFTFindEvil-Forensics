1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------
Host rd01 (192.168.1.5, user: fredr) was actively under a coordinated RDP
brute-force campaign at the time of memory acquisition. Two external IPs —
81.30.144.115 and 213.202.233.104 — generated 75+ sequential connection
attempts against the host's exposed RDP service (TCP 3389), with a third IP
(81.19.209.101) joining mid-campaign. At least four concurrent ESTABLISHED
RDP sessions were present during/after acquisition, confirming successful
authentication.

Independently, a suspicious 32-bit executable — MRC.exe — was launched from
a non-standard staging location (D:\Tools\MRC.exe) by the interactive desktop
(explorer.exe, PID 7464) approximately 83 seconds before the Windows.info
system timestamp. Windows Defender SmartScreen checked the executable 37
seconds before launch, indicating it was newly introduced to the system.

No memory injection artifacts were detected by windows.malfind. The RDP
TermService host (svchost.exe PID 1248) is confirmed legitimate. The attacker
appears to have gained interactive access via RDP credentials rather than
through in-memory code injection.

The host should be treated as fully compromised. Credentials known to this
host must be rotated, and all active RDP sessions from the identified IPs must
be terminated immediately.
